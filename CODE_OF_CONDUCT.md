# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sustained disruption,
publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainer via
[a private security advisory](https://github.com/nguyenan97/angular-saas-kit/security/advisories/new)
or a direct message. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may
edit, hide or delete contributions, and may block accounts, for behaviour
judged inappropriate.

This is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
